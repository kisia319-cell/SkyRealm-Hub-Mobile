**Important:** these are sound effects, not prerecorded Russian dialogue or professional voice-actor performances. The previous Windows/WebView text-to-speech dialogue has been removed. SFXMint identifies these effects as AI-generated or procedurally synthesized and publishes them under CC0. The game retains its locally generated radio static/interference if the remote sound host is unavailable; the published MP3 cues require an internet connection.
